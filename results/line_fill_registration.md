# Registration: is the line-first rune effect the greedy line fill? (2026-10-30, before the check)

**Trigger.** §64 left the line-first effect as a property of the first glyph on a line. The page scans (scream314/cicada3301, assets/2014/liber-primus-complete, 75 JPEGs, SHA-256 recorded outside the repository) show the runes are a digital font with fixed per-rune widths (I 9 px, the stem runes 34, the 53-px class U C P E M D Y IA, X 66, EA 87, standard deviation 0–3 px), that line-initial glyphs have exactly the height and the per-rune width of the same runes elsewhere (439 aligned lines, no first glyph more than 1 px from its rune's width), and that lines break mid-word two times in three. A text filled greedily, breaking a line when the next glyph does not fit, starts the next line with the glyph that did not fit, and a wide glyph fails to fit more often than a narrow one in proportion to its width. That selects wide runes for line starts without any cipher or transcription step.

**Checks** (`work/line_fill.py` on the scans, outside the repository; its output copied into `results/line_fill.md`). G1, the fill rule: on every aligned page, the right-hand slack of a line (the page's widest line end minus this line's end) is smaller than the next line's first glyph width plus one inter-glyph space, for at least 95 % of line pairs; under a fill that breaks at words or by hand the slack is unconstrained by the next glyph. G2, the selection: the expected count of rune r at line starts is proportional to its count on the pages times (w_r + s), s the inter-glyph space measured on the scans; the 29-cell χ² against that prediction is to fall below 42 (p > 0.05 on 28 df) where it is 78.7 against the pages' own distribution. Expected: G1 and G2 hold, and the effect is a layout mechanism, closed. If G1 holds and G2 fails, the width model is incomplete (word-boundary breaks, separators) and is refined once, stated; if G1 fails, the lines were not greedily filled and the effect goes back to §64's open readings.
