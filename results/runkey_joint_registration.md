# Registration: English running keys as a class, a library-free joint decoder (2026-10-31, before any run)

**Gap.** Every running-key lane here (§6, §9, §25, §32, §36, §37, §40, §53, §55, §61) and in the ledgers (pnotisdev about 200 texts, Dukotah 33, wazar's esoterica) names the key text and tries it. If the key is an English text nobody has named, those lanes have no power whatever its length. After the §47 correction the English running key is the one additive family the S weighting is compatible with (6 % S at lag-5 coincidences, the pages' 11 of 102 at p 0.03–0.09), which makes the class worth testing as a class. A running-key cipher whose plaintext and key are both English is breakable without the key text: at each position the 29 choices of plaintext fix the key rune, and two English n-gram models score the two streams jointly; a beam search over the pair of contexts recovers both streams at a few hundred symbols. No ledger row shows such an attack on the pages.

**Instrument** (`src/runkey_joint.py`). Beam search (width 2,000, duplicate states merged) over positions with state = the last four plaintext runes and the last four key runes; at each position every plaintext rune p is scored by the English 5-gram (`ngram.load_dense`, trained on the twelve-text corpus, Bunyan held out) and the implied key rune by the same model: sub k = c − p, add k = p − c, beaufort k = c + p. The soft reject-on-repeat rule is modelled exactly: a skip branch consumes the one key rune that would have reproduced the previous cipher rune, k″ = c_(i−1) − p under sub, at cost log P(k″ | key context) + log 0.81. Literal F on and off (an F in the cipher may be a plaintext F that consumed no key). The decoder returns the joint score, the plaintext and the key. Fillers (§59) are not modelled in this registration; their effect is measured as a power statement.

**Null and acceptance.** The joint score's null is the same decoder on uniform random pages, 200 at each of five lengths (120, 180, 230, 260, 277) for each F setting, mean and standard deviation interpolated to a page's length; the three signs share a null by symmetry. A page is a candidate if its joint z ≥ 6, above the largest null score at its length, its decoded plaintext's rune 5-gram z is at least half the plants' median, and it reads by eye; the decoded key's 5-gram z is reported beside it.

**Power stage, first.** 30 plants: Bunyan plaintext at 262 runes, Bunyan key from a segment at least 20,000 runes away, sub sign, soft rule 0.81, no fillers, F off; six more under add and six under beaufort; 30 more with the §59 filler form at p = 0.40. Reported: rune accuracy of the decoded plaintext, the joint z, the plaintext and key 5-gram z.

**Claims.** J1: without fillers the decoder recovers at least 0.8 of the plaintext runes on at least 15 of the 30 plants (power ≥ 0.5 at page length). J2: the 55 pages × 3 signs × 2 F yield no candidate. The filler plants are a statement, not a claim. **Expected: J1 holds, J2 holds.** If J1 fails the instrument is below power at page length, the pages are still run and reported, and no claim about the class is made beyond the measured power.

**Budget.** Plants minutes; null about an hour; pages a quarter of an hour.
