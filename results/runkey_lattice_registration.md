# Registration: the word-lattice joint decoder for English running keys (2026-10-31, before any run)

**Why, and what the first instrument showed.** The joint decoder of `results/runkey_joint_registration.md` scores both streams with the rune 5-gram and fails at page length for a reason the diagnostic made exact: on every plant its best explanation scores 130–240 nats *above* the true path, so the search is sound and the model is the limit; two English 5-gram streams admit fake decompositions the model prefers to the truth. The pages carry a constraint that decoder ignores: word separators. If the plaintext is English with those separators, each plaintext word is a dictionary word of a known rune length (or, in the §59 world, one rune shorter with one filler). A lattice of dictionary words on the plaintext side with the 5-gram on the key side removes most fake decompositions, and nobody's ledger shows a dictionary-constrained running-key attack on the pages.

**Instrument** (`src/runkey_lattice.py`). Dictionary: the 18,580 rune-spelled word types of the twelve-text corpus (Bunyan held out; it covers 96 % of Bunyan's tokens), weighted by log frequency. For each word slot of the page (the transcription's separators) the candidates are the dictionary words of that rune length; in the **filler arm** also the words one rune shorter with one filler rune at any position, the filler consuming no key, at the free-null penalty −2.2. The key stream is scored by the 5-gram with the exact skip branch per rune, taken greedily (skip when log P(k″ | ctx) + log 0.81 + log P(k | ctx, k″) beats log P(k | ctx), k″ the rune that would have reproduced the previous cipher rune). Literal F on and off. Beam of 300 key-context states after every slot, duplicates merged. Three signs. Output: joint score, plaintext words, key runes.

**Null and acceptance.** Null: the same decoder on 100 random pages per (F, arm) with the real pages' own word structures (one random page per real page, lengths kept), signs sharing by symmetry; z from the null at the page's own structure pooled by rune count. Candidate: joint z ≥ 6, above the null maximum, plaintext 5-gram z at least half the plants' median, readable by eye; the key's 5-gram z reported beside it.

**Power stage, first.** 30 Bunyan plants with Bunyan's own word boundaries (262 runes, key from a segment ≥ 20,000 runes away, sub, soft 0.81, no fillers) under the exact arm; 30 with the §59 filler form at p = 0.40 under the filler arm; 6 add and 6 beaufort. Reported: word accuracy, rune accuracy, joint z, plaintext and key 5-gram z.

**Claims.** L1: word accuracy ≥ 0.8 on at least 15 of the 30 exact-arm plants. L2 (statement): the filler arm's power. L3: the 55 pages × 3 signs × 2 F × 2 arms yield no candidate. **Expected: L1 holds; L3 holds.** If L1 fails the instrument is below power, the pages are still run and reported, and no class claim is made beyond the measured power.

**Budget.** Plants minutes to an hour; null and pages about an hour on eight cores.
