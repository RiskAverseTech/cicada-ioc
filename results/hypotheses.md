## What the loop found

All three proposers converged on the same mechanism: 55 of the 58 constructions carry a soft reject-on-repeat rule at p ≈ 0.81, so the doublet column passes nearly everything and the gate separated families on the other three statistics; the deliberate probes (hard rule, bump, never-repeating pad, plaintext autokey) failed exactly where phase 2 said they would, which is a consistency check on the gate. 45 distinct constructions pass the pre-registered rule, 24 of them also pass the strict reading. Three groups. **(1) Stream plus ciphertext feedback** (K_i = map(C_(i−1)) + S_i with S a totient, prime or running-text stream; the top ranks of Claude and GPT and most of round 2): 22 of 24 pass the pre-registered rule but only 7 pass strict, because the lag-1 difference stream under feedback is P + S and inherits the base stream's non-uniform residues (lag-1 class χ² 65–96 against the pages' 42, four to eight null sigmas); wazar's 48 feedback configurations cover the additive form. **(2) Position-walking affine multipliers** (C = ((i mod 28)+1)·P + K, the 'clock' idea from the field survey) over any stream lift the unigram χ² to 40–50 and the lag-1 χ² to 55–75; with the two published word keys DIVINITY and FIRFUMFERENFE the fits are strict 2.5 and 4.9, and nobody has run them under the skip rule (812 configurations each). **(3) Independent streams with no text feedback** (LCG mod 3301, mod 1033 and mod 29 seeds, an LCG mod 2³¹−1, the uniform probes) pass both rules trivially: an independent key with the right rejection rate *is* the phase 2 survivor, and the gate cannot tell a Cicada-flavoured LCG from a one-time pad; only sweep size distinguishes them. The closest strict fits of the run (1.7–1.8) are the Gematria-prime-value ciphertext feedback over the book's own running text and the LCG mod 29 (a 28-periodic key), both slightly above the uniform family on the lag-1 column, where the pages themselves sit two sigma above uniform; 1.7 against 2.3 is not a significant difference. The usable output is the short list above: small finite families that no ledger names (the LCG mod 3301 / 1033 / 29 seeds, the two published word keys with the clock multiplier, prime-value feedback over the book's text), each a few thousand to a few million decoder tests; the 2³¹-seed LCGs on the same list are not sweepable blind and are kept only for completeness.

What the loop did not find: any construction that reproduces the pages by a route other than an independent key with a soft reject-on-repeat. Every text-coupled key (feedback, autokey) that passes does so weakly and is distinguishable on the lag-1 column with the strict reading; every uncoupled key passes regardless of its source. The brief's conclusion stands and the panel did not break it.
