**Reading.** The repeats fall inside words 60 times against 64.3 expected and at word boundaries 21 times against 17.3; every boundary class sits within one sigma of boundary-blind, and their positions within pages are uniform. A chain or stepping cipher that resets at word, line or page starts, and a transcription artefact at line ends, would both have put the repeats at boundaries. Neither did. The anti-repeat mechanism acts uniformly on the stream, which is what a filtered pad or any boundary-blind generator does.
