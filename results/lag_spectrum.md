Lags 2–120: mean z -0.17, sd 0.98, extremes -3.1 (lag 11) and +1.8 (lag 60); 1 of 119 lags at |z| ≥ 3 (0.3 expected by chance). The deficit is at lag 1 only.

**Boundary convention.** Within pages: 86 doublets in 12,901 pairs. Counting the 54 page joins as adjacent pairs: 86 doublets in 12,955 (0 at the joins, 1.9 expected if the joins were random). No page-boundary convention can move the count by more than the number of joins it adds or removes, so the deficit of 86 against about 445 does not depend on the 55-, 56- or 58-page convention; retranscription would have to change hundreds of runes to matter, and the two independent transcriptions on record (rtkd/iddqd and Leo-Y-Zhang's cross-check) agree to within a few.
**The null model, stated.** Two expectations for adjacent repeats: 12,901 pairs / 29 = 445 under independent uniform runes, and 12,901 × Σ p_i² = 446 under independence with the OBSERVED rune frequencies (index of coincidence 0.0346); the figure "447" quoted elsewhere is the second with a slightly different pair count. Against either, 86 is a deficit of about 17 sigma. **Per-page uniformity, stated.** The 55 per-page doublet counts against each page's share of 86: chi² 58.5 on 54 degrees of freedom (README §21 premises reported 58.9 on 55), i.e. the suppression is the same on every page to within noise; 14 pages have zero doublets against 13.5 expected. The panel's "uniformity is unestablished" was answered in §21 before it was asked.
